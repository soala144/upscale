# UPSCALE: full pitch script (word for word)

Total: about 8 minutes. Spoken parts are plain text. Stage directions are in [brackets]. Timings are approximate.
Speak slowly. Pause after every number. If you run short on time, cut Section 5 first.

---

## 1. Opening (0:00 to 0:40)

[Look at the judges. Smile. Do not start with your name.]

"Quick question. How many of you have messaged a business and never got a reply?

[Pause. Let them react.]

Keep that feeling in mind.

Picture Ada. She sells handmade leather bags from Lekki. A customer sees her Instagram post at nine at night and messages her: 'How much is the big bag?'

Ada is asleep. Or she is serving someone else. Or she simply cannot reply right now. By the time she answers the next morning, that customer has already bought from someone who replied in two minutes.

Ada does not have a product problem. She has a reply problem. And Ada is not rare."

[Say your team and product name now.]

"We are [team name], and this is UPSCALE."

---

## 2. The problem (0:40 to 2:00)

"Nigeria has about 39.6 million small businesses. That is the NBS and SMEDAN survey, as reported by PwC. They employ close to nine in ten working Nigerians and produce nearly half of our GDP.

And according to GSMA research, over half of the Nigerian small businesses that sell online do it only through social media, WhatsApp, Instagram and Facebook. For these businesses, the entire shop floor is a chat window.

Chat windows are unforgiving.

Harvard Business Review audited 2,241 companies by sending each one a real enquiry. Twenty-three percent never replied. Another twenty-four percent took more than a day. The average reply took forty-two hours.

In a separate Harvard study of 1.25 million leads, companies that responded within an hour were about seven times more likely to qualify the lead than companies that waited just one hour longer. And more than sixty times more likely than companies that took a day or more.

MIT research found that contacting a lead within five minutes, instead of thirty, made it about twenty-one times more likely to be qualified.

[Pause.]

Most of those numbers come from American web enquiries. Now picture one person running a shop, answering messages between customers. Speed is not a nice-to-have for them. It is the whole game."

---

## 3. The solution, pain by pain (2:00 to 4:15)

"So we asked owners like Ada what actually hurts. They gave us five things.

**One. 'I reply too late.'**
UPSCALE puts an assistant on your Telegram that answers in seconds, at any hour, in your voice. It is not a menu of buttons. It is a conversation, like a good shop assistant: it greets the customer by name, asks what they need, recommends something that fits, and tells them the price.

**Two. 'A bot will not know my business.'**
That is what our Knowledge page is for. You add your products, prices and answers, one by one or by uploading a spreadsheet. The assistant quotes only what you told it. If it is not in there, it does not guess. It says, 'let me check with the team.'

**Three. 'I cannot tell who is serious.'**
Every conversation is quietly qualified: what they need, their budget, their timeline, and whether they make the decision. Each lead gets a score, hot, warm or cold. Ada opens her dashboard and knows who to call first.

**Four. 'My leads are everywhere.'**
In chats, in a notebook, in people she met at an event. UPSCALE keeps them in one place. She can add a lead by hand, or import a spreadsheet. Or she puts a link in her Instagram bio or her ad. A customer taps it and the chat starts, or fills a short form and continues on Telegram with their details already attached. Every link carries a label, so she can see which ad actually brought customers.

**Five. 'Sometimes it has to be me.'**
With one click, Ada takes over any conversation, replies from her own bot, and hands it back to the assistant when she is done.

And once a lead is warm, the rest of the job is there. She can message past leads who have agreed to hear from her. She can book calls and visits on a calendar, linked to each lead. And the dashboard shows revenue only from confirmed payments, never from pending ones."

## 4. Live demo (4:15 to 7:00)

"Let me show you instead of telling you."

[Phone on the left, laptop on the right. Say each line, then do the action.]

1. [On the phone, send "hi".]
   "I am the customer. I say hi."
   [Reply appears.]
   "Notice it used my first name, told me in one line what the shop sells, and asked what I am after. No wall of text."

2. [Send "what do you have in store".]
   "Now I ask what they have. A short overview of the categories, not the whole catalogue."

3. [Send "I need something to carry my laptop, not too expensive".]
   "I tell it what I need. It recommends a fit and quotes the price. That price came from the Knowledge page. Nobody typed it into the chat."

4. [On the laptop, open Leads, then open that lead.]
   "Here is that same customer as a lead. The full transcript, and what the assistant picked up on its own: what I need, my budget, my location."

5. [Click Take over. Type a reply. Send.]
   "Now I am the owner. I click Take over. The assistant stops. I type a reply."
   [Show the phone.]
   "There it is, from the shop's own bot. When I am done, I click Hand back to AI."

6. [Open Lead capture.]
   "These are the links Ada pastes into her bio. One opens the chat. One is a form."
   [Open Leads, click Import CSV, upload the demo file.]
   "And this is a spreadsheet of leads from a trade show. One click."
   [Read the result.]
   "It imported the new ones, skipped the duplicate, and told me exactly which rows had problems."

7. [Open Overview.]
   "And this is everything at a glance. Leads, how many qualified, how many converted, revenue from confirmed payments, and the pipeline. All from real data."

[If anything fails: "That is the live system, not a recording." Move to the next step. Do not apologise.]

## 5. Why not HubSpot (7:00 to 7:25)

"People ask, why not HubSpot? HubSpot is built for a marketing team with forms and email. Our customer sells in chat, answers alone, and pays in naira. UPSCALE starts where their customers already are, replies for them, and costs between three and a half and ten thousand naira a month. We are not trying to be a better HubSpot. We are the sales assistant a one-person shop never had."

## 6. What is next, and the close (7:25 to 8:00)

"We are honest about where we are. Everything you just saw is live. Next on our roadmap is automatic follow-up, so no lead goes quiet, and WhatsApp, where most Nigerian customers already are. WhatsApp needs business verification and approved message templates, and we have designed for that from day one. Our leads, conversations and broadcasts do not depend on Telegram, so adding it is one new connector, not a rewrite.

[Pause. Slow down.]

Customers do not leave because your product is bad. They leave because nobody answered.

UPSCALE makes sure someone always does.

Thank you."

## Delivery notes

- Practise the opening until you can say it without looking down. It sets the whole tone.
- Say each statistic once, then its source. Do not stack three numbers in one breath.
- Eye contact during the Ada story and the close. Look at the screen only during the demo.
- Rehearse the demo twice before you go on. Have the Telegram chat already open and the demo CSV file ready.
- If a judge interrupts, answer in one or two sentences and return to where you were.
- If you are cut to 5 minutes: drop Section 5 and shorten Section 3 to the first three pains and the dashboard.

## Quick answers if asked

- **How does it know my business?** It is not retrained. Each message carries your Knowledge page entries and instructions. Change a price and the next reply uses it.
- **Can it lie about prices?** It is told to quote only what is listed and to say it will check with the team otherwise.
- **Is my data safe?** Every request is scoped to your organization from your signed-in session. Bot tokens are encrypted. People who say stop, or block the bot, are excluded from broadcasts.
- **Business model?** Basic ₦3,500, Growth ₦5,000, Scale ₦10,000 per month, with a 14-day trial.
- **What is not done?** Automatic follow-ups, appointment reminders, WhatsApp, and customer payment links, which wait on Bachs enabling Connect for our account.
